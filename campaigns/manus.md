## Mandatory rule: exclude prior Manus partners
Exclude creators who previously partnered with Manus. Public absence is not proof; direct
relationship clearance is required before outreach. Uncertain stays blocked.

Detection (verified 2026-10-03): creator-specific links of the form `manus.im/redeem?c=<CODE>` in
public video descriptions, "sponsored by Manus", #ad or a YouTube paid-promotion flag. Many creators
with Manus videos carry such codes; a Manus video with no code is Unverified, not clear. Aggregator
(e.g. SponsorRadar) reports are provisional until the description is read. Search the YouTube
surface for "<creator> Manus", read the matching video descriptions, record URL and date.
